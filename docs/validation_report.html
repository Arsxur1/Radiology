Not a clinical validation

All figures come from a synthetic generator and small, pathology-enriched subsets of open datasets — this is engineering validation of the pipeline (finding detection + measurement accuracy), not a clinical study. Subset specificity is understated (subsets are enriched with pathology). CardioLens is a decision-support tool, not a medical device. Critical findings always escalate to a physician.

Per-class performance

PTB-XL — 704 records

Sensitivity and specificity per finding, sorted by sensitivity. Specificity is scored against all other records in the subset, so secondary ST-T changes from LVH/BBB count against ST classes even when clinically real.

ST classes read at J+60 ms (SCP-ECG convention); st_depression specificity is understated — 83% of its false positives are LVH/BBB strain, contextualised downstream by the causal layer, not primary ischemia.

Interval bias (measured − gold) and mean absolute error against per-beat expert wave-boundary annotations.

PR raw bias −34 ms drives a pipeline calibration of +34 ms (not a threshold change). QRS is the energy estimator. QT is bounded near ~32 ms by the physiological ambiguity of the T-offset — the tangent method did not beat it.

Method

  • HarnessSource-agnostic: a labelled case = signal + gold findings (+ gold intervals) run through the full pipeline; per-class sens/spec computed.
  • ScoringEach dataset scores only the finding codes its label map can supply — no always-zero rows.
  • SubsetsStratified by SCP/SNOMED, up to ~90/class; pathology-enriched, so specificity is a lower bound.
  • BeatsVentricular ectopy from a transparent LDA on the MIT-BIH inter-patient split (de Chazal DS1→DS2), JSON coefficients, no runtime ML.
  • DelineationAccuracy measured on LUDB; PR calibrated from it; QRS width replaced with a slope-energy estimator.
  • Honesty (§0)Detectors that don't separate on real data are documented and dropped, never shipped to pad coverage.
